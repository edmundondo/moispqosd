Movitel
Mobile · no published current subscriber count
Joint venture of Viettel Group (Vietnam, state-owned) and SPI Gestão e Investimentos (Mozambican company); exact ownership split unconfirmed. Has the widest rural/district coverage of any operator (127+ district centers) plus its own fiber backbone. No published current subscriber count found in this research pass.

Moçambique Telecom, S.A. — 100% Mozambican state-owned. Formed January 2019 from the merger of TDM (fixed) and Mcel (mobile); these are not two separate current companies. Offers mobile, fixed-line voice and fixed broadband. No published current subscriber count found in this research pass.
