Vodacom Moçambique
Mobile · customers (company-published, FY2025, as of 31 March 2025)
Majority owned by Vodacom Group (South Africa; itself majority-owned by Vodafone Group plc, UK); local shareholders include Intelec Holdings and the Emotel consortium (exact stakes unconfirmed). Offers 2G/3G/4G/5G mobile, fixed broadband and M-Pesa mobile money. Company-published figure: 12.4 million customers as of 31 March 2025 (FY2025 results) — not an INCM-verified count.

Movitel
Mobile · no published current subscriber count
Joint venture of Viettel Group (Vietnam, state-owned) and SPI Gestão e Investimentos (Mozambican company); exact ownership split unconfirmed. Has the widest rural/district coverage of any operator (127+ district centers) plus its own fiber backbone. No published current subscriber count found in this research pass.

Mobile · no published current subscriber count
Moçambique Telecom, S.A. — 100% Mozambican state-owned. Formed January 2019 from the merger of TDM (fixed) and Mcel (mobile); these are not two separate current companies. Offers mobile, fixed-line voice and fixed broadband. No published current subscriber count found in this research pass.

TVCABO Moçambique
Fixed/Wireless · no published current subscriber count
Majority-owned by Visabeira Group (Portugal); a 2024 report referenced Visabeira 'seeking an 80% stake', implying it wasn't yet fully consolidated at that level — exact current stake unconfirmed. Offers fiber/cable broadband and pay-TV, primarily in urban areas (Maputo, Beira, Matola, Nampula). No published subscriber count found in this research pass.
